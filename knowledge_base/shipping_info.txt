StyleSync Shipping Information
================================

DOMESTIC SHIPPING (Pakistan):
- Standard Shipping: 3-5 business days | Rs. 150
- Express Shipping: 1-2 business days | Rs. 350
- Free Standard Shipping on orders over Rs. 3,000

INTERNATIONAL SHIPPING:
- South Asia (India, Bangladesh, etc.): 7-10 business days | $8 USD
- Middle East: 10-14 business days | $12 USD
- Europe / North America: 14-21 business days | $18 USD
- Free international shipping on orders over $80 USD

ORDER PROCESSING:
- Orders are processed within 1-2 business days of placement.
- Orders placed after 3 PM PKT are processed the next business day.
- During sale events, processing may take up to 3 business days.

TRACKING YOUR ORDER:
- A tracking link is sent to your email once your order is dispatched.
- You can also track your order at stylesync.com/track using your order number.
- Tracking updates every 12-24 hours.

SHIPPING DELAYS:
- Delays may occur during public holidays, sale events, or extreme weather.
- If your order is delayed beyond the estimated delivery window, contact support@stylesync.com.
- We will investigate with the courier and provide an update within 24 hours.

FAILED DELIVERY:
- If a delivery attempt fails, the courier will retry once more.
- After 2 failed attempts, the package is held at the local depot for 5 days.
- If unclaimed, the package is returned to StyleSync and a re-delivery fee applies.

CUSTOMS & DUTIES (International):
- International customers are responsible for any customs duties or import taxes.
- StyleSync is not responsible for delays caused by customs clearance.
