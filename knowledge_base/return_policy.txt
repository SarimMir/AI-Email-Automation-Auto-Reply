StyleSync Return & Exchange Policy
====================================

RETURN WINDOW:
- Items can be returned within 30 days of delivery.
- Items must be unworn, unwashed, and in original packaging with tags attached.
- Sale items are final sale and cannot be returned.

EXCHANGE POLICY:
- Exchanges are accepted within 30 days of delivery for a different size or color.
- Exchange processing takes 3-5 business days once the item is received.

REFUND PROCESS:
- Approved refunds are processed within 5-7 business days to the original payment method.
- Shipping costs are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error (wrong item, defective product).

HOW TO INITIATE A RETURN:
- Email support@stylesync.com with your order number and reason for return.
- Our team will send a prepaid return label within 24 hours.
- Drop the package at any courier drop-off point.

DAMAGED OR DEFECTIVE ITEMS:
- If you received a damaged or defective item, contact us within 48 hours of delivery.
- We will send a replacement at no extra charge or issue a full refund.
- Please include photos of the damage in your email.

WRONG ITEM RECEIVED:
- If you received the wrong item, contact us immediately with your order number and a photo.
- We will arrange a free return and expedite the correct item.
