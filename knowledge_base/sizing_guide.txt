StyleSync Sizing Guide
=======================

WOMEN'S SIZES:
XS  → Bust: 32", Waist: 24", Hips: 34"
S   → Bust: 34", Waist: 26", Hips: 36"
M   → Bust: 36", Waist: 28", Hips: 38"
L   → Bust: 38", Waist: 30", Hips: 40"
XL  → Bust: 40", Waist: 32", Hips: 42"
XXL → Bust: 42", Waist: 34", Hips: 44"

MEN'S SIZES:
XS  → Chest: 34", Waist: 28"
S   → Chest: 36", Waist: 30"
M   → Chest: 38-40", Waist: 32"
L   → Chest: 42-44", Waist: 34"
XL  → Chest: 46-48", Waist: 36"
XXL → Chest: 50-52", Waist: 38"

FIT GUIDE:
- Our clothing runs true to size unless noted otherwise on the product page.
- For a relaxed fit, we recommend sizing up one size.
- For bodycon/fitted styles, size down if you are between sizes.
- Denim: Our jeans are true to waist size. If you have wider hips, size up.
- Knitwear: Sizing runs slightly smaller — we recommend sizing up.

HOW TO MEASURE:
- Bust/Chest: Measure around the fullest part of your chest.
- Waist: Measure around your natural waistline (smallest part).
- Hips: Measure around the fullest part of your hips.

SIZE CHART AVAILABILITY:
- Every product page has a "Size Guide" button with product-specific measurements.
- If you are unsure, email us at support@stylesync.com and share your measurements — we will recommend the best size.
