04. Retrieval-Augmented Generation
ChromaDB RAG Knowledge Base Retrieval

📑 Knowledge Base Documents

Indexed 21 paragraph-level vector chunks from enterprise files:

  • return_policy.txt — 30-day rules & process
  • sizing_guide.txt — Size charts & fit guide
  • shipping_info.txt — Delivery rates & hubs

🎯 Context Injection Mechanism

Dynamic cosine similarity retrieval injects top-3 passages directly into the draft prompt.

Result: 0% Policy Hallucinations; 100% factual accuracy in drafted responses.

05. Prompt Engineering
Documented Multi-Stage Prompt Pipeline

PT-001: Intent Classifier

Produces strict JSON outputs with confidence distribution, urgency level, and sentiment tags.

PT-002: Reply Drafter

Generates brand-aligned replies incorporating RAG context & customer sentiment.

PT-003: Tone Adjuster

Rewrites drafts dynamically into Formal, Casual, Empathetic, or Concise tones.

PT-004: Action Checklist

Outputs actionable operational steps for human support agents upon approval.
